In Your Phase — Windows (64-bit)

VST3:        copy the folder "In Your Phase.vst3" to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
Standalone:  "In Your Phase.exe" runs from any folder.

Windows SmartScreen may warn because the files are not code-signed:
choose "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW. In Your Phase is listed under "moonshroom studio".

---

Deutsch: Den Ordner "In Your Phase.vst3" nach C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
kopieren. Die .exe läuft aus jedem Ordner. Warnt SmartScreen, "Weitere Informationen"
und "Trotzdem ausführen" wählen. Danach in der DAW die Plugins neu scannen.
