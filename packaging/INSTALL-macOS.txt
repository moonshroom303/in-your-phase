In Your Phase — macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer)

Audio Unit:  copy "In Your Phase.component" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
VST3:        copy "In Your Phase.vst3"      to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
Standalone:  copy "In Your Phase.app"       to Applications

The builds are ad-hoc signed, not notarized. If macOS blocks them after
downloading, remove the quarantine flag once in Terminal:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"In Your Phase.component"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"In Your Phase.vst3"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/"In Your Phase.app"

Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW. In Your Phase is listed under "moonshroom studio".

---

Deutsch: Die .component nach ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, die .vst3 nach
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/, die App in den Programme-Ordner kopieren. Blockiert
macOS die Dateien nach dem Download, die Befehle oben einmal im Terminal ausführen.
Danach in der DAW die Plugins neu scannen.
